Methodology

Step 1: Python requests and BeautifulSoup automatically extract website signals.

Step 2: The extracted facts are converted into structured JSON data.

Step 3: A rule-based scoring system evaluates basic website completeness.

Step 4: The structured facts are provided to an LLM manually for judgment and recommendations.

Step 5: AI recommendations are reviewed against the actual website before being treated as final findings.

Human Double-Check: Automated checks cannot fully determine design quality, legal content accuracy, real mobile responsiveness, usability, or the quality of client experience. These areas should be manually reviewed.
